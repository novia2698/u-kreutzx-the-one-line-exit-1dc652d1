The aim is not just a valid entry but a line good enough to land inside the paid top 10 — judged blind, in pairs, so the line has to beat other funny lines head to head, not merely pass the gate.

What this entry does: main.ts writes one fixed line, 127 characters, with no trailing newline, no network, no clock and no randomness, so two runs are byte-identical.
